# XYZ Pay-per-km: concept prototype

A clickable concept prototype for the Nagarro case assessment (Sukhpal Singh Mann, ISB PGP Co 2027).

- **Guided demo:** 12 steps through Anshika's year, from comparing cover at renewal to her refund and renewal offer. Each step says what happens and what it proves for XYZ. Use the arrow keys or tap the highlighted button.
- **Try your numbers:** the refund rule, the year-end calculation and the claim routing rules, live.

XYZ Insurance is fictional. Anshika, Priya and Ananya are illustrative, as are the car, the dates and every rupee figure. Prices are before GST. No data leaves the page and no payment is taken.

Live: https://sukhpalmann.github.io/XYZ-PayPerKm/
